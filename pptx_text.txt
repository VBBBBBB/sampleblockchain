--- Slide 2 ---
Problem Identification & Motivation
Current Challenges
Significant leakage and black marketing in distribution networks
"Ghost beneficiaries" systematically siphoning subsidized rations
Opaque supply chains lacking end-to-end visibility
Traditional web portals rely on centralized databases with absolute administrator control
Centralized Vulnerabilities
SQL/NoSQL databases create single points of failure. Administrators can tamper with data undetectably, compromising integrity and auditability.
Our Solution
Replace centralized databases with a distributed, immutable ledger ensuring end-to-end traceability and preventing double-dipping through cryptographic validation.

--- Slide 3 ---
Related Work & Industry Benchmarks
Blockchain in SCM
Saberi et al. (2019) established the value of shared, immutable "chain of custody" for supply chains, providing foundation for our approach.
Hyperledger Fabric vs. Public Chains
Ethereum's volatile gas fees and public visibility make it unsuitable for government use. Fabric's Execute-Order-Validate architecture fits enterprise needs perfectly.
Walmart Food Trust Benchmark
Modeled after Fabric-based system that reduced traceability time from 7 days to 2.2 seconds—demonstrating real-world scalability.
Academic Foundation
Builds upon theoretical "e-Ration" proposals (Jain et al., 2021) by implementing full-stack, working solution with production-ready architecture.

--- Slide 5 ---
Blockchain Layer: The Foundation
Hyperledger Fabric v2.5
Permissioned blockchain utilizing Raft Consensus (Crash Fault Tolerant) across three distinct organizations: Government, District, and Fair Price Shop.
Execute-Order-Validate Architecture
Transactions execute first, then order, then validate—ensuring high throughput while maintaining consistency. Membership Service Provider uses X.509 certificates for identity.
CouchDB State Database
Rich state-based queries enable instant retrieval of specific district allocations without full ledger scans—critical for audit operations.

API Middleware Layer Design
Singleton FabricService
Maintains persistent gRPC connections to Gateway instead of reconnecting per request—dramatically improving efficiency.
Wallet Management
Handles identity management via cryptographic wallets, abstracting blockchain complexity from application logic.
Transaction Submission
Submits transactions via Fabric SDK with proper endorsement policies and consensus handling.
Clean code structure organized into Configurations, Controllers, Services, and Models—ensuring maintainability and testability.
